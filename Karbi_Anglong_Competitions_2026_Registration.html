75th Karbi Anglong Day 2026 Competitions

📋 Competition Rules & Information

Common Eligibility & Registration

  • Open to students of any class/age group from schools under KAAC.
  • Quiz: 2 participants per team.
  • Debate: 1 participant per team/school.
  • Painting: Any number of participants may join (no restriction).
  • One student cannot participate in more than one event/team.
  • Valid School ID Card is compulsory.
  • Students must produce a valid School ID Card at the venue.
  • Registration is online only. On-the-spot registration will not be accepted.
  • Participants must report at least 30 minutes before the event starts with their registration confirmation.

Prizes

  • 1st Prize: ₹5,000 with medal and certificate
  • 2nd Prize: ₹3,000 with medal and certificate
  • 3rd Prize: ₹2,000 with medal and certificate

Quiz Rules

  • Format: Preliminary Round (Written) → Round of 10 → Elimination Round → Final Round.
  • Syllabus: General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Science, History, Sports, Northeast India, Karbi Anglong History & General Knowledge.
  • Medium: English.
  • Time per question: 30 seconds.
  • Scoring: Direct question – 10 marks; Passed question – 5 marks; No negative marking.
  • Use of mobile phones, notes or audience help is strictly prohibited.
  • Marks awarded by the Quiz Master/Judges shall be final and binding.
  • In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round followed by a sudden-death question will decide the winner.
  • Only 2 participants per team are allowed.

Debate Rules

  • One participant per school/team.
  • Topic: Are Sports as Important as Education?
  • Time: 4 minutes speech + 1 minute rebuttal.
  • Medium: English (or Karbi/English as permitted).
  • Participants must adhere strictly to the time limit.
  • Marks awarded by the Judges shall be final and binding.
  • Misbehaviour or use of unfair means will lead to immediate disqualification.

Painting Rules

  • Any number of participants may join (no restriction on the number of participants per school).
  • Topic will be given on the spot.
  • Time: 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Drawing sheet (chart paper) will be provided by the Organisers.
  • Participants must bring their own colours, brushes and other materials.
  • The painting must be original. Tracing, stencil, copying, mobile phone or any external help is strictly prohibited.
  • Name, School and Roll Number must be written clearly in bold letters only on the back side of the painting.
  • Marks awarded by the Judges shall be final and binding.
  • Each participant will be judged individually.

Discipline & General Rules

  • Participants must maintain discipline and respect the Judges and Organisers at all times.
  • Misbehaviour, argument, copying or use of unfair means will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Audience help will attract deduction of marks.
  • The Organisers reserve the right to modify the rules, schedule or cancel the event in case of unavoidable circumstances.
  • By registering online, participants agree to abide by all the rules and regulations.
